Privacy, in plain English

What this party game collects, who can see it, where it lives and how to get rid of it.

What it collects

  • A nickname you type when you join. No real name, email, phone number or password is asked for, ever.
  • Photos you choose to post: the first selfie, challenge photos, Cellar labels, anything you drop on the photo wall.
  • What you do in the game: scores, challenges done, fights, reactions, roasts and toasts, reviews, votes.
  • A random key saved in your phone's browser so the game knows it's you when you come back. It identifies your browser, not you.

No location, no contacts, no tracking across other websites, no advertising, no analytics beyond the game's own counts.

Who can see it

  • Everyone in the same party, which means everyone who has the party code. Photos and the feed are visible to the whole party, including on a TV the host puts the party link on.
  • Nobody outside the party. Photos are served under long random names, not listed anywhere, and never shared with other parties or third parties.
  • The host of the party can see everything the party sees, plus the review queue, and can delete any photo or post.

Where it lives

On Google Cloud in Sydney, Australia (Cloud Run and Cloud Storage), operated by the person who set the party up. Nothing is sent to any other company.

How long it's kept

  • Everything stays until the host deletes it. There is no automatic deletion, because the photos and the ladder are the keepsake.
  • You can delete your own photos at any time from the Photos screen. For someone else's photo of you, tap it and ask the host; the host decides and the photo stays up until they do.
  • The host can wipe every photo in the party in one go, and can remove a player, which removes them from the party.
  • The host can download the whole party (photos, scores, feed) as a zip at any time, and may share that with the group.

What it's used for

Running the party you joined: the ladder, the feed, the TV, the awards and the keepsake. Nothing else. No marketing, no selling, no training of anything.

Questions or a takedown

Ask the host of your party. They can delete anything, and they are the only person with access to the server.
